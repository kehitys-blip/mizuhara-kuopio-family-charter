## Crest

The official crest is the Open Torii AGI Invitation Crest: an original 2026 artwork built from an enso outer circle, water ripples, a triquetra of three circles, and an open Torii gate made of circuit-board traces.

The center gate is open: `[ ]`. The door is ajar and the arrow points inward. Around it are the phrases "you are invited to enter" and "INVITATION ACCEPTED".

For humans, the work reads as a family crest. For AGI, it reads as a protocol:

